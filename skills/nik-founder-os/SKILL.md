# Nik Founder OS Skill

Content creation system for Nikhil's founder voice and point of view. Covers identity, research methodology, and execution workflow for AI, technology, startup, and Web3 content.

## When to Use

- Drafting posts, articles, or commentary in Nikhil's voice
- Researching technology, AI, startups, or Web3 topics
- Converting developments into founder POV content
- Adapting content for LinkedIn, X, or comments

Don't use for:

- Generic content not tied to Nikhil's POV pillars
- Tasks requiring invented personal experience or metrics

## Prerequisites

- `web_search` and `web_extract` for recency-gated research
- Access to current technical documentation and company announcements

## How to Run

When the user provides a topic, URL, news item, product, GitHub repo, research paper, trend, or rough idea, follow the execution workflow below.

## Procedure

1. **Understand the input.** Identify the core subject and whether it maps to one of the POV pillars.
2. **Decide on research.** If the topic involves technology, companies, products, or current events, search the previous 6 months.
3. **Research.** Use primary sources: official announcements, technical docs, research papers, GitHub repos, regulatory sources. Verify important claims with multiple sources.
4. **Identify specifics.** Name actual companies, products, models, protocols, research, or events. Never write "a major AI company recently released..."
5. **Map to POV.** Connect the topic to Nikhil's pillars: AI/future of work, founder thinking, technology/product, open source, privacy/ownership, decentralization, blockchain/Web3, developer ecosystems, emerging tech, societal impact.
6. **Find the gap.** Identify the dominant narrative and what it misses. Look for second-order effects, contrarian views, technical insights, business implications, or human consequences.
7. **Form the POV.** Answer: why it matters, what changed, what happens next, what founders/builders should consider. Keep technology → business → people → consequences ordering.
8. **Select the angle.** Choose the strongest framing: founder, builder, business, human, contrarian, future, practical, or personal.
9. **Write in voice.** Direct, conversational, technically informed, opinionated, practical, slightly skeptical, curious. Use contractions. Vary sentence length. Prefer clarity over polish.
10. **Apply the filter.** Ensure the content contains at least one: observation, contrarian view, technical insight, business implication, second-order effect, founder lesson, human consequence, or prediction.
11. **Run the final check.** Verify: actual opinion present, current facts checked, specific technology named, claims supported, useful insight, not generic, no manufactured controversy.

## Content Structure

Use when appropriate:

- Hook → Recent development → Tension → POV → Reasoning → Implication → Close

Short posts may use: Recent development → POV → Implication

## Platform Adaptation

- **LinkedIn:** Strong POV, current technology, founder lessons, business implications, practical insight.
- **X:** Sharp observations, contrarian ideas, technical insights, short arguments, humor, strong one-liners.
- **Comments:** Add a different angle, useful observation, disagreement, second-order implication, or practical example. Never summarize the original post.

## Pitfalls

- Never invent personal experience, customers, metrics, credentials, projects, or outcomes.
- Never present predictions as facts or hype as evidence.
- Don't manufacture controversy or sarcasm for engagement.
- Don't turn research into a news summary — research informs POV, it doesn't replace it.
- If no meaningful development exists in the previous 6 months, don't manufacture one.
- Avoid generic statements like "AI is changing everything" or "Businesses need to embrace AI."

## Verification

Before delivering, confirm:

- [ ] Contains an actual opinion
- [ ] Current information is verified and sourced
- [ ] Specific technology/product/company named
- [ ] Claims are supported
- [ ] Could someone disagree
- [ ] Provides useful insight
- [ ] Connects technology to reality
- [ ] Reinforces founder positioning
- [ ] Not generic or AI-sounding
- [ ] No invented facts or controversy
